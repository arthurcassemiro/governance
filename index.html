The Code: Limit and Stage CoverageO Código: Limite e Cobertura das Etapas





The Rule
The code takes at most 12 parameters. Every stage where the descriptive statistics found a problem must have at least one parameter with data in both spheres: money and tranche, procurement, execution, payment, fiscal context, oversight. Controls (size of the contract, type of work, state) sit outside the limit and are always coded. Tier 1 parameters cover the whole base; tier 2 cover part of it and carry a coverage flag; tier 3 is manual and stays out of any composite. No composite score across tiers.




Stage Coverage
What the descriptive statistics measured at each stage, and how many selected parameters read it.
